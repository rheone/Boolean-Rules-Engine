Core Semantic Invariants

An implementation conforming to this specification SHOULD preserve these invariants:

1. **Every logical expression produces a K3 value.**
2. **Unknown is not implicitly converted to False.**
3. **Unknown is not implicitly converted to True.**
4. **Predicates produce K3 values.**
5. **Cardinality uses the `[definitely true, possibly true]` interval.**
6. **Projection/collapse is explicit.**
7. **Inspection does not collapse the enclosing expression.**
8. **Coalesce replaces Unknown but does not otherwise alter K3 values.**
9. **Derived operators have definitions in terms of semantic primitives.**
10. **N-ary operators use their explicit n-ary semantics rather than accidental binary folding where those semantics differ or are materially clearer.**
11. **Boolean evaluation is a boundary operation, not an alternate global evaluator mode.**
12. **The semantics of implication are explicitly Strong Kleene material implication (`¬A ∨ B`).**
